Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about IGC HK Exam

1

How to register an account for IGC HK Exam?

IGC HK Exam is a member-based website and can only be registered by invitation only. If you would like to join, please email wjec@igchkshop.dpdns.org for enquiries.

2

Why do I have to sign in for IGC HK Exam?

As all the content is copyrighted under IGC HK Exam, we would like to protect our customers and our own business. Hence only users with authorization can sign into our website.

3

Would my password be seen by IGC HK Exam administrator?

No, IGC HK Exam does not have access to any user's password as it is safely stored in a database by Firebase, which is run by Google. IGC HK Exam administrators can only reset your password by sending a template email to your registered email address, but we cannot access your password.

4

Are the information accurate?

IGC HK Exam cannot guarantee all our information is correct and accurate. However, these materials are thoroughly checked and monitored by professionals. All we can say is the materials are the best they could be.

5

What happens if I forgot my password?

IGC HK Exam currently does not have an automatic password reset system. In order to reset your password, please contact our administrator or email: wjec@igchkshop.dpdns.org

Need More Help?

If you have additional questions not covered here, please don't hesitate to contact us at wjec@igchkshop.dpdns.org. We typically respond within 24-48 hours.
